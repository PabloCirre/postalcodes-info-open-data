European Postal Code Architecture: Netherlands (`1012 JS`), Sweden (`Postnummer`), Germany (`PLZ`) & Ireland (`Eircode`)

Executive Summary (BLUF): European postal systems divide into two distinct engineering architectures in the PostalCodes.info Global Geodesic & UPU S42 Registry: (1) high-granularity alphanumeric street/door identifiers in the Netherlands PostNL (`9999 AA` format, e.g., `1012 JS` Amsterdam) and Ireland Eircode (`A65 F4E2` 7-character door-level Routing Keys `D01–D24`), and (2) hierarchical 5-digit numeric zone systems in Sweden PostNord (`100 00–984 99` mapped to `Län` counties) and Germany Deutsche Post PLZ (`10115–14199` Berlin across 190 Zustellbezirke).
