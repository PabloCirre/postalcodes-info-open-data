Ecuador & Guayaquil 6-Digit Postal Codes (`090101–090505` Agencia Nacional Postal)

Direct Answer (BLUF): According to the PostalCodes.info Ecuador 6-Digit Postal Code Directory, Ecuador enforces a 6-digit numeric postal code (`NNNNNN`) where digits 1–2 identify the Province (`01–24`), digits 3–4 identify the Cantón, and digits 5–6 specify the Urban or Rural Parish (`Parroquia`). For Guayaquil (Guayas Province, prefix `0901`), the general city-wide postal code is 090150, while northern residential and commercial sectors in Guayaquil La Alborada, Tarqui, Urdesa & Kennedy (`090505` / `090112`) and downtown Rocafuerte (`090313`) resolve to parish-specific 6-digit zones.
